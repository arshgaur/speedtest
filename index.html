How Our Speed Test Works

Our internet speed test measures the connection speed between your device and our servers. Here's what happens when you click "Start Test":

  1. Ping Test: Measures the latency of your connection (how quickly you get a response).
  2. Download Test: Measures how fast data can be transferred from our server to your device.
  3. Upload Test: Measures how fast data can be transferred from your device to our server.

For best results, close other applications using your internet connection during the test.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good internet speed?

Good internet speed depends on your usage. For basic browsing and email, 5-10 Mbps is sufficient. For HD video streaming, you'll want at least 15-25 Mbps. For 4K streaming or multiple devices, 50+ Mbps is recommended.

Why is my upload speed slower than download?

Most internet plans are asymmetrical, meaning they provide faster download speeds than upload speeds. This is because typical internet usage (like streaming or browsing) requires more download bandwidth than upload.

How can I improve my internet speed?

Try these steps: restart your router, move closer to your WiFi router, disconnect unused devices, check for background downloads, or contact your ISP about upgrading your plan.
